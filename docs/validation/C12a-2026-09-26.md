## Performance observations and limits

Foreground warm full-build observations on the same synthetic vault: accepted C11 **6.272 s**, C12a **6.599 s** (about 5.2% higher in this pair). Both used zero body reads, zero parser calls and 79 body-cache batch lookups. C12a's sampled heartbeat maximum was 79.6 ms, p95 29.8 ms; the accepted sample was 99.3/30.4 ms. This is one observation per version, not a statistical benchmark. Sampled renderer heap before/after was approximately 949/1,239 MB for C12a versus 818/1,208 MB for C11; these are not peak memory measurements and differing initial heaps prevent causal conclusions. Heartbeat gaps are not paint latency. C00's physical-device/cold-performance matrix remains open.

An across-reload full-node hash differed, so that probe was not credited as equality; no specific cause was established. The same-input actual accepted implementation comparison above resolved semantic compatibility. Initial explanation probes used nonexistent root note paths and were excluded. Occluded, initializing and overlapping diagnostic runs were excluded from timing comparison, including the cleanup rebuild with 19 hidden samples. One intermediate strict URL patch timing check failed at 70.6 ms under load; the final full suite passed with unchanged thresholds. CLI newline escaping and the deletion harness expectation were corrected without production changes: existing deletion intentionally retains a ghost and tag hierarchy until later collection/rebuild. C14/C15 retain incremental revision/publication and create/delete-race work.

The 256-record cap bounds count, not raw bytes. The revision fence and digest cover structural facts, not a complete atomic snapshot of other families. Temporary pending endpoint records rely on this collector's bounded encounter order; they are not a generic arbitrary-source deferred-edge store.
