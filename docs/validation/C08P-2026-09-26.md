## Reviewed corrections

The delivered watchdog needed stronger lifetime guarantees. Review extended its scope to metadata/targeted preview reads, guarded publication after awaited search/rebinding, made terminal diagnostics immutable, retained the last active phase/progress timestamp and added sampled search/resolver progress. Replaced/unloaded runs settle immediately and clear their watchdog. Destruction also invalidates graph-build generations. Startup/coordinator continuations check unloading after relevant awaits so cancellation cannot start a replacement build or register late startup work.

No snapshot schema, settings keys, graph semantics, parser grammar, worker strategy or iOS policy changed. No injected delay, runtime test controller or diagnostic console stream ships. A watchdog recovers stalled asynchronous work; it cannot interrupt a synchronously blocked or suspended renderer.

- Warm comparison uses three explicit snapshot restores after initial startup has settled, on the same fixture and semantic settings. It measures the restore call through full hydration, **not total Obsidian startup or first paint**. Both versions performed zero Markdown body reads, zero full builds and zero patches. The accepted HEAD baseline and tested C08P artifact hashes identify the runs. A preliminary concurrent-startup measurement hung and was excluded; a later final-build fault run also encountered a hidden renderer before its evidence phase, so its total call duration includes a scheduling pause and is not a normal warm timing; the host was reset before the controlled comparison.
- Fallback is a full graph build with a warm parsed-body cache, **not a cold parse of 1.9 GB of Markdown**. A representative no-op patch is measured separately. Sampled JavaScript heap is an observation, not process peak/RSS or a retained-memory regression proof. Final foreground warm runs were 6.159 / 5.745 / 5.487 seconds (median 5.745 seconds), versus 6.569 / 5.801 / 4.735 seconds (median 5.801 seconds) for accepted HEAD: no material warm regression in this small sample. The final fallback build took 15.225 seconds with zero body reads; the final fault fired after 94.337 seconds of evidence-phase inactivity. Total wait was 246.407 seconds because the renderer had been hidden before entering that phase. A representative one-file no-op patch took 279.7 ms, made one body read, performed no full build and emitted no semantic event. Exact timings/counters appear in the JSON report.
