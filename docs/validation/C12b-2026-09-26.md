Both had zero body reads, zero cached body reads, zero parser calls and **79** body-cache batch lookups. Final heartbeat maximum/p95 was **102.6/30.5 ms**; C12a was 79.6/29.8 ms. No hidden samples occurred in the final measurement. Sampled final heap before/after was approximately 1,279/1,710 MB, versus C12a 949/1,239 MB; differing initial heaps and non-peak samples prevent causal/peak-memory conclusions. This is an observed increase, not proof of performance parity or a statistical benchmark. All strict portable timing checks passed unchanged.
